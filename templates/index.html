How FoodBridge Works

🏨

1. Donor Posts Food

Hotels, restaurants, or event organizers post excess untouched food with location, quantity, and expiry details.

🛵

2. NGO Accepts Pickup

Nearby NGOs or volunteers browse live listings, claim items, and view interactive maps to pick up food promptly.

🍲

3. Status Tracked & Delivered

Real-time status updates ensure transparency from Available to Picked to Delivered to community shelters.
